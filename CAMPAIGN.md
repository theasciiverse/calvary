# Calvary: The Golgotha Transmission (campaign draft)

Forty missions, as Crack dot Com intended ("over 40 cities all over the
world"). The premise and the three heroes are theirs, from the 1997 story
pages recovered in `references/Golgotha/crack-com-story/`. Everything past
that teaser is new writing for Calvary, built to fit what the original team
left behind: the 15 music tracks, the regional texture sets (Iraqi marsh,
Egyptian sand, Greek coast, Roman, Swiss and Scandinavian snow, French,
industrial "crom", lava, castle, temple, a small alien/moon set) and the
landmark models (Jerusalem walls, temple and dig site, the Giza pyramids, a
French castle, cathedral beams, Swiss skiers).

Status: draft for discussion. Nothing here is built yet except the three
existing maps it reuses (marked **existing map**).

## The story

**What the original gives us.** 2048. Global nuclear disarmament, and the
murder of a beloved American archaeologist, lead to an American incursion
into Iraq; Europe answers with its own military power play, and World War
III begins. Commander John Fisher II, leading the American force, questions
his orders, abandons his country and takes his troops on a quest for the
truth, uncovering a supernatural conflict behind the politics. With him are
Lieutenant Elaine Kruese and Sergeant Reid Charles. Years later, Kruese
faces a press conference: what really happened at the Golgotha site back in
'48?

**What Calvary adds.**

- *The archaeologist* is Dr. Ruth Calloway. Golgotha means "place of the
  skull". Digging beneath it in 2047, she found one: human in shape, three
  times too large. Over the following year she traced the same giant
  remains under forty sites across Europe, the Middle East and the north,
  each one older than the city above it. Her notebook calls them by the
  name Genesis 6 gives them: the Nephilim.
- *The murder* is staged to look like an Iraqi killing, and it works: the
  incursion begins. The real killers are the **Watchers' Order**, a cabal
  inside the European military command named for the fallen angels of the
  Book of Enoch who fathered the Nephilim. They do not want the war won;
  they want it fought, on top of the forty sites, because the bloodshed is
  the key. The war is a ritual at continental scale.
- *Fisher* finds Calloway's notebook in her abandoned camp at Nakhayb
  (mission 4), sees his own orders are routing him from one site to the
  next, and at Nineveh (mission 7) turns his column around. From then on
  the player fights both armies: the Americans hunting a deserter and the
  Europeans hunting the notebook.
- *Kruese* tells the story. Each act opens with a fragment of her press
  conference, the "Golgotha Transmission" of the original's Act 1 Scene 1,
  and the last mission ends there.
- *Reid Charles* is the sergeant who keeps the column alive: engineer,
  scrounger, the one who reads Calloway's handwriting. He is the player's
  voice on the radio between missions.
- *The relics.* The Watchers' Order is not only fighting over sites but
  collecting what lies at them: the Shroud at Turin, the Holy Lance in
  Vienna's treasury, the Veil at Chartres. Fisher's column gets there first
  some of the time, and every relic it saves is one the ritual lacks.
- *The end.* At Svalbard Fisher learns the forty sites are a lock and
  Golgotha the keyhole. In Norway the first Nephilim wake. The last act
  returns to Jerusalem, under the dig, to close what Calloway opened.

Tone: the original's own epigraph is Shakespeare's prophecy of "the field
of Golgotha and dead men's skulls". The holy sites are what the heroes
defend, never what the game mocks; the enemy is the cabal and what it is
trying to wake.

## Missions

Each line: **site**, terrain set, music, then the mission and what it
teaches or adds. Music titles are the original 15 tracks; regions without
a track of their own borrow the nearest one.

### Act I: The Incursion (Iraq)

*Transmission: "We went in because they told us an American was dead. She
was. They lied about who killed her."*

1. **Al Basrah**: Egyptian sand + crom industrial; Al Basrah. Beachhead.
   Supertank only: drive, shoot, rearm at the HQ.
2. **Shatt al-Arab marshes**: marsh set; Al Basrah. Cross the
   waterways; first garage, peon tanks, the bridger.
3. **Ur**: sand + temple; Nakhayb. Calloway's first dig, under the
   ziggurat. Banks and money planes; hold the site.
4. **Nakhayb**: sand; Nakhayb. Her abandoned camp. Engineers capture
   the camp; the notebook is found.
5. **Babylon**: sand + ruins; Nakhayb. The second site; the first giant
   bones on screen. Electric cars and towers.
6. **The Euphrates line**: marsh + sand; Al Basrah. Convoy defence
   while orders change twice in one night.
7. **Nineveh**: ruins + sand; Nakhayb. Fisher refuses the order to raze
   the site and turns west. First fight against both armies.

### Act II: The Desertion (Levant and Egypt)

*Transmission: "After Nineveh we had no flag. We had a notebook and a
sergeant who could read it."*

8. **Palmyra**: temple + sand; Cairo. Rearguard among the columns while
   the US pursuit closes in.
9. **The King's Highway**: sand; Cairo. Long escort south; the road to
   Jerusalem is closed, and Reid says "not yet".
10. **Sinai**: sand; Cairo. Desert crossing; rocket tanks; supply by
   money plane only.
11. **The Suez Canal**: sand + water; Cairo. Bridger mission: hold the
   crossings while the column passes.
12. **Cairo**: **existing map** `egypt2`; Cairo. Street war in the walled
   town; first helicopter.
13. **Giza**: sand + pyramid models; Cairo. A site beneath the plateau;
   the Watchers' Order shows itself for the first time (black-banded
   units that fight for neither side).
14. **Alexandria**: sand + coast; Cairo. Seize ships for Greece under a
   jet strike.

### Act III: The European War (Mediterranean to the Alps)

*Transmission: "Europe was not fighting America. Europe was fighting on
schedule."*

15. **Piraeus**: **existing map** `test` (Greek coast, harbour wall);
   Greece. Landing and the full battle as it plays today.
16. **Delphi**: Greek + temple; Greece. The oracle's site; capture the
   heights, superguns introduced.
17. **The Ionian crossing**: Greek coast; Greece. Islands and air:
   bombers.
18. **Naples**: lava set (Vesuvius); Naples. Fight on the volcano's
   flank as the site under it stirs; lava damages vehicles.
19. **Pompeii**: ruins + lava ash; Naples. Night mission in the ash;
   the first relic race.
20. **Rome**: Roman set; Rome. Siege of the city's hills; tank busters
   and cobras.
21. **Turin**: crom industrial; Turin. Factory city and the Shroud;
   rescue it before the Order's convoy leaves.
22. **The Gotthard Pass**: snow; Zurich. Mountain road, tunnels,
   avalanche terrain.
23. **Zurich**: **existing map** `snow` (lake valley); Zurich. The banks
   of Zurich, literally: capture them to fund the north.
24. **Chartres**: French set + cathedral beams; Turin (France has no
   track of its own; the release's `charte_france` module is in a tracker
   format we cannot decode yet). Defend the cathedral and the Veil.
25. **The Loire**: French + castle; Turin. Assault on the Order's
   chateau (the `francecastle` model).
26. **Munich**: crom industrial; Munich. Armour factories; destroy the
   Order's tank line.
27. **Vienna**: castle + Roman; Vienna. The Holy Lance in the treasury;
   a race through the old city.

### Act IV: The North

*Transmission: "Svalbard is where we stopped calling them 'sites'."*

28. **Berlin**: crom industrial; Munich. The Order's command, found empty;
   their map shows forty points and a line north.
29. **The Baltic crossing**: snow + sea; Helsinki. Ice and ships; air
   cover only from carriers.
30. **Helsinki**: snow + crom; Helsinki. Harbour city battle; the
   Finnish army joins Fisher.
31. **The Finnish lakes**: snow + water; Finland. Lake country manoeuvre,
   bridgers over the narrows.
32. **Lapland**: snow; Finland. Polar night, fog of war, reindeer herds
   (bird and animal props).
33. **The fjord**: snow + sea; Norway Nephelim Battle (quiet opening).
   Cliffs and a single road; supertank duel with the Order's own.
34. **Svalbard**: snow + crom; Norway Nephelim Battle. The vault under
   the mountain holds Calloway's last letters; the lock and the keyhole.
35. **The Nephilim Battle**: snow + alien crust; Norway Nephelim Battle.
   The first giants wake under the ice. Hold until the ice breaks.

### Act V: Golgotha

*Transmission: "You want to know what we found? We found that she was
right."*

36. **Cyprus**: Greek coast; Greece. Staging for the last voyage;
   rebuild the army with everything saved so far.
37. **Jaffa**: sand + coast; Jerusalem. Landing under fire from all
   three sides.
38. **The walls of Jerusalem**: Jerusalem walls + temple models;
   Jerusalem. Hold the city's walls, do not damage them (holy sites are
   no-fire zones: a scoring rule, not a gimmick).
39. **The dig**: `jerusdig` model, temple; Jerusalem. Calloway's trench
   under Golgotha; engineers clear the way down.
40. **Beneath the place of the skull**: alien crust + moon set;
   Jerusalem, then the splash theme. The keyhole. Close it.

*Epilogue: the press conference. "What really happened at the Golgotha
site, Lieutenant?" Kruese looks into the cameras. Fade to the spinning
globe of the original's opening, and the stars.*

## Build notes

- Three missions reuse the existing maps (12 Cairo, 15 Piraeus,
  23 Zurich); 37 need new maps. That needs a terrain format of our own
  (heightmap + tile names, readable by the same renderer) and a small
  terminal map editor, both still to design.
- The bridger (missions 2, 11, 31) depends on decoding the original's Lisp
  variables (bridgeable path nodes). Lava damage, avalanches, holy-site
  no-fire zones and the relic races are new mechanics to design.
- Briefings: Kruese's transmission fragments open each act, Reid's radio
  lines frame each mission; the original radio voice clips cover the
  battle barks.
- Music: 14 of the 15 tracks are placed; the splash theme closes the game.
