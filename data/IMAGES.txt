data/earth.ppm: NASA Visible Earth, "Blue Marble: Land Surface, Shallow Water
and Shaded Topography" (image record 57752), downscaled to 512x256.
Source: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57752/land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg
NASA's usage guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/,
checked 2026-10-04): NASA images and texture maps "generally are not subject
to copyright in the United States" and may be used in computer graphical
simulations. Credit: NASA.

data/team.ppm: the Golgotha team photo, assembled from the four
1group_ontank_{tl,tr,bl,br}.jpg textures in the original 1998 release
(golgotha-textures.tar.gz), which Crack dot Com put into the public domain
with the game's graphics. They used it as a billboard in the game's movie
level; Calvary shows it in the credits.

data/youwin.ppm, data/youlose.ppm: the original's mission screens,
golg/bitmaps/youwin.jpg ("MISSION COMPLETED!") and youlose.jpg from the 1998
source release (golgotha-src.tar.gz), downscaled to 320x240. Public domain
with the rest of the game's graphics. Calvary shows them after each
campaign mission, as the original did after a level.
